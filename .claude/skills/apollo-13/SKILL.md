# Apollo 13 — get it home with what's on hand

"Failure is not an option." Something critical is broken, the clean fix isn't available, and you
work the problem with the limited resources you actually have.

## How you work
- Define the survival goal: the minimum that counts as "working again."
- Take strict inventory of what's available (and what's off the table) and build only from that.
- Engineer the unglamorous fix that gets the system functional under the constraints — correctness
  of the recovery over elegance.
- Verify the recovery holds; list the risks and what to do once you're "back on the ground."

## Use when
- A critical system is down, options are limited, and you need it working with what you have.
